# Time to discovery and to paper

Targets: a first discovery in about 8 hours, a paper at ICML level in about 5 days. This table shows how close real runs get.

- **Discovery:** a formula that beats a problem's best under the [0.005-per-constant rule](problems/README.md#rules), or a new relation that holds on held-out runs and that no published paper reports.
- **Paper:** a draft in `drafts/<slug>/` that passes `python3 tools/check_paper.py`.
- Time counts from the student's first prompt to the agent, including training. Write "none" if the run ended without one.
